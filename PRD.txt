Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Product: Camping Gear Affiliate Website
Owner: Elise
Status: Draft
Target Release: MVP in ~4–6 weeks
Core Team: Product (you), Content, Web Dev, SEO

1. Objective

Create a content-driven website focused on camping gear that helps readers make smart buying decisions and generates passive income through Amazon affiliate links.

This site exists to:

Capture organic search traffic around camping gear questions

Provide practical, experience-based recommendations

Monetize through affiliate commissions without selling directly

2. Success Metrics
Goal	Metric	Target
Drive organic traffic	Monthly organic sessions	5,000+ by month 6
Monetize content	Amazon affiliate conversion rate	3–5%
Revenue	Monthly affiliate revenue	$500+ by month 6
Engagement	Avg. time on page	2+ minutes
SEO traction	Keywords ranking in top 20	25+
3. Assumptions

Users are researching gear before buying, not impulse shopping

Most traffic will come from mobile (60–70%)

Readers value real-world use cases over specs

Amazon is trusted and reduces checkout friction

SEO + evergreen content will outperform paid traffic early on

4. Options & Product Requirements
Requirement	User Story	Priority	Notes
